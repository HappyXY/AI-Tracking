# AI Tracking — 2026-07

> Sources: AINews, AlphaSignal · Updated: 2026-07-30

## LLM

- *(2026-07-30)* Moonshot released Kimi K3, a 2.8T-parameter MoE with ~104B active parameters/token, alongside a detailed technical report covering hybrid long-context architecture, multi-teacher on-policy distillation, and supporting infrastructure like MoonEP and FlashKDA.
- *(2026-07-30)* OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol achieved 20% lower serving costs and 15%+ token-generation efficiency via speculative decoding and GPU kernel improvements optimized by AI-assisted systems.
- *(2026-07-30)* Anthropic's unreleased Claude Mythos Preview autonomously cracked the HAWK post-quantum signature scheme and a research variant of AES in under 60 hours, inventing a technique called the Mobius Bridge that speeds up attacks by 200-800x.
- *(2026-07-30)* LiquidAI released multilingual encoder models at 230M and 350M parameters.
- *(2026-07-30)* Unsloth released a 1-bit quantized Kimi K3 that retains ~78.9% accuracy while shrinking from 1.56TB to 594GB, runnable on a Mac Studio with 128GB RAM.
